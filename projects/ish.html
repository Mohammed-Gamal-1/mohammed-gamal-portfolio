01 / THE IDEA

The idea behind
the build.

ISH brings together perception, voice services and home-safety monitoring in an intelligent spherical robot concept. The team explored how camera input, voice commands, IoT sensors, alerts and a mobile interface can operate as a connected system.

Smart-home functions often live in separate devices. ISH explores a more connected approach: a robot that can observe its environment, recognize people and make its sensing and assistant capabilities accessible through shared services.
